About Sickle Cell

Sickle Cell Disease can cause painful crises, fatigue, and other complications. Early symptom tracking and prompt care can reduce risks. This platform is a guide, not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Stay Hydrated

Water helps reduce crises frequency. Keep a bottle handy.

Keep Warm

Cold can trigger pain episodes. Dress in warm layers.

Know Your Triggers

Track what worsens symptoms—stress, dehydration, cold, infections.
